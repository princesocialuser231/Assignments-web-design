2. Recipe: How to Make Tea (Ordered List)

  1. Boil 2 cups of water in a pan.
  2. Add 1-2 teaspoons of tea leaves.
  3. Add sugar to taste.
  4. Pour in 1/2 cup of milk.
  5. Let it boil for 2-3 minutes on medium flame.
  6. Strain into a cup and serve hot.
